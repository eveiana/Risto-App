# 🦁 Risto Expo Snack Guide

You can now instantly load and run your **Risto** storytelling app in the **[Expo Snack](https://snack.expo.dev/)** compiler tool! We converted your views and components into standard React Native primitives (`<View>`, `<Text>`, `<ScrollView>`) styled using highly portable `StyleSheet` rules and native vector configurations.

---

## 🚀 Speedruns Setup in Expo Snack

1. **Visit Expo Snack**: Open [https://snack.expo.dev/](https://snack.expo.dev/) in your computer browser.
2. **Clear the default Boilerplate**: Under your project tab in the panel on the left, click on the **`App.js`** file.
3. **Copy-paste the conversion code**: Open `/EXPO_APP.js` from this AI Studio workspace, copy its entire contents, and paste it override-wise into your Snack's `App.js` file.
4. **Choose your Preview Screen**: Over in the right-side emulator preview pane, select your platform preference — **Web**, **My Device** (which scans a QR code using your own phone's Expo Go app), **iOS**, or **Android**.

---

## 📦 Bundled Packages Used
These packages are automatically matched and injected by Expo Snack, making them fully operational out of the box:
* `react`: React core primitives hooks.
* `@expo/vector-icons`: Icon assets including `Feather`, `Ionicons`, and `FontAwesome` (used for playback items, menu toggles, search glyphs, etc.).
